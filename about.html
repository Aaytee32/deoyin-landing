About

Small studio.
Serious domains.

Deoyin & Company is an independent studio building specialized software for the parts of life sciences and healthcare where getting it wrong has real consequences.

Why we exist

Regulated industries are underserved by generic software.

Many SaaS are built for the broadest possible market. That's a poor fit for HEOR analysts running CEA models, RA/QA teams tracking global regulatory change, or lab managers who need calibration and audit trail data to hold up under inspection. We build narrow, deep tools for these teams instead of asking them to bend generic workflow software into shape.

01

Compliance is a feature

Audit trails, sign-and-lock records, and validation-friendly workflows are core requirements in every platform we ship, not an afterthought.

02

Depth over breadth

Each product solves one problem well (health economic modelling, regulatory intelligence, signal detection) rather than trying to do everything.

03

Built by practitioners

Every platform is shaped by direct conversations with the analysts, scientists, and QA leads who use it daily.

Life sciences, quality systems, operations.
